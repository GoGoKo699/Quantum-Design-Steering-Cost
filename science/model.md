# Model, question, and exact optimum

A measurement on one half of an entangled pair can prepare many different pure-state ensembles on the other half. When the measuring apparatus must conserve charge, its available phase coherence constrains those ensembles. The question here is quantitative: how much initial reference coherence is necessary and sufficient to prepare a state 2-design on a system that is never acted on?

The answer is an attained minimum. For a system of dimension $D$ with charge $Q_A$ and infinite-temperature charge variance $V_A$, the minimum reference quantum Fisher information is

$$
\boxed{F_{Q,\min}=4D V_A.}
$$

The target is some weighted exact complex projective 2-design. The optimization includes every finite reference state and every finite conserving measurement in the model below. It permits a mixed reference, arbitrary collective interactions, and consumption of the reference. The [reference bound](reference_bound.md) proves necessity; the [count-and-phase construction](conserving_construction.md) proves finite attainment.

## 1. Entangled source and conserved charge

Let $A$ and $B$ each have dimension $D$. Choose paired orthonormal bases and write

$$
Q_A=\sum_{i=1}^{D}q_i|i\rangle\langle i|,
\qquad
Q_B=-Q_A^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
|\Omega\rangle_{AB}
=\frac{1}{\sqrt D}\sum_{i=1}^{D}|i\rangle_A|i\rangle_B.
$$

Transposition refers to these bases. The source has a sharp total charge:

$$
(Q_A+Q_B)|\Omega\rangle=0.
$$

Its two marginals are maximally mixed. Additive scalar shifts of the charges change the sharp total value but leave all commutators, variances, and QFI costs unchanged.

Define

$$
\mu=\frac{\mathrm{Tr}Q_A}{D},
\qquad H_A=Q_A-\mu I_A,
\qquad V_A=\frac{\mathrm{Tr}H_A^2}{D}.
$$

The spectrum may contain degeneracies. Arbitrary finite real $q_i$ are allowed. The symmetry generated by a general real spectrum is the real-parameter group of time translations. A compact $U(1)$ description requires a common charge lattice for $A$, $B$, and the apparatus, after scalar shifts and a common choice of charge units. The attaining reference automatically shares the system lattice when one exists. The proof requires exact commutation with the charge, not a nondegenerate or equally spaced spectrum.

The reference $R$ is finite dimensional, with Hermitian charge $Q_R$ and initial density operator $\rho_R$. The complete input is

$$
|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|_{AB}\otimes\rho_R.
$$

Independence of $R$ from $AB$ is part of the task. Correlating an auxiliary resource with the source changes this input model.

## 2. Allowed measurement and retained ensemble

Only $BR$ is operated on. An allowed finite-outcome measurement has effects

$$
M_x\geq0,\qquad
\sum_xM_x=I_{BR},\qquad
[M_x,T]=0,\qquad
T=Q_B\otimes I_R+I_B\otimes Q_R.
$$

Each effect is invariant individually. The outcome labels form a neutral classical record: the symmetry does not rotate or relabel them. Every outcome is retained, including any outcome that a device might otherwise call failure. A zero-probability effect may be included to complete a POVM on unoccupied input support.

The unnormalized retained state on $A$ is

$$
\tau_x
=\mathrm{Tr}_{BR}\!\left[
(I_A\otimes M_x^{1/2})
(|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\otimes\rho_R)
(I_A\otimes M_x^{1/2})
\right].
$$

Set $p_x=\mathrm{Tr}\tau_x$ and $\rho_x=\tau_x/p_x$ for positive-probability outcomes. The remote state depends on the effect alone, so this expression does not restrict the disturbance of the discarded apparatus. Completeness gives

$$
\sum_xp_x\rho_x=I_A/D.
$$

This fixed average is essential. Conditioning on a selected subset of outcomes generally changes it and defines a different optimization.

Every invariant finite POVM used here has a finite dilation by a charge-conserving unitary and a neutral pointer. The construction gives this dilation explicitly. Thus the effect model has an implementation in which preparation and readout of the pointer introduce no additional charge asymmetry.

Classical randomization is allowed. Its choice can be included in the neutral record. All asymmetric ancillary states, controllers, and accessible preparation flags are included in $R$. In particular, if a classical flag selects a coherent reference preparation and remains accessible to the apparatus, the resource is the QFI of the flagged state. Discarding that flag only in the accounting would undercharge the actual input.

## 3. Charged resource

For the family $\rho_\theta=e^{-i\theta Q}\rho e^{i\theta Q}$, use symmetric logarithmic derivative quantum Fisher information. If
$\rho=\sum_a r_a|a\rangle\langle a|$, then

$$
F_Q(\rho,Q)
=2\sum_{a,b:\,r_a+r_b>0}
\frac{(r_a-r_b)^2}{r_a+r_b}
|\langle a|Q|b\rangle|^2.
$$

The charged resource is the initial value

$$
F_R=F_Q(\rho_R,Q_R).
$$

For a pure state, $F_Q=4\mathrm{Var}(Q)$. For a mixed state, charge variance can include classical fluctuations and is not interchangeable with QFI. The converse below uses the QFI of the actual mixed reference.

Charge units remain fixed when comparing protocols. Rescaling all charges by a factor $a$ rescales both $V_A$ and $F_R$ by $a^2$, preserving the stated law.

The reference can be consumed, correlated with discarded systems, or disturbed. No return condition is imposed. Apparatus dimension, mean charge or energy, interaction time, number of outcomes, locality, classical computation, and gate complexity are not included in this QFI optimization. The finite construction establishes attainability without asserting optimality for those other resources.

## 4. Exact design target

Let $\mathsf S$ exchange two copies of $A$. The Haar second moment of normalized pure states is

$$
M_{\mathrm H}=\frac{I+\mathsf S}{D(D+1)}.
$$

A finite ensemble with probabilities $p_x$ and pure states $\rho_x$ is a weighted complex projective state 2-design when

$$
M_2:=\sum_xp_x\rho_x\otimes\rho_x=M_{\mathrm H}.
$$

This tensor equality fixes every polynomial of bidegree $(2,2)$ in the state amplitudes. Its partial trace also fixes the first moment to $I/D$. It does not require a continuous Haar distribution, equal probabilities, a prescribed number of states, or a specified SIC or set of mutually unbiased bases.

The exact equality itself forces purity if mixed $\rho_x$ were initially allowed. Indeed,

$$
\sum_xp_x\mathrm{Tr}\rho_x^2
=\mathrm{Tr}(\mathsf S M_2)=1.
$$

Every density operator has purity at most one. Hence every positive-weight output is pure. This observation makes the exact optimization unambiguous even when the general measurement model permits mixed conditional states.

For the centered charge, the design identity gives

$$
v:=\sum_xp_x[\mathrm{Tr}(H_A\rho_x)]^2
=\frac{V_A}{D+1}.
$$

The charged apparatus must therefore create a pure ensemble whose conditional charge means fluctuate on this scale.

## 5. Main theorem and proof route

**Exact design cost.** Fix $D<\infty$ and $Q_A$. Minimize $F_Q(\rho_R,Q_R)$ over finite-dimensional independent reference states, reference charges, and finite invariant POVMs satisfying Sections 1–4. Then the minimum exists and equals

$$
\min F_Q(\rho_R,Q_R)=4D V_A.
$$

The theorem follows from two complementary statements.

First, any allowed protocol, including one with mixed outputs, obeys

$$
\sum_xp_xF_Q(\rho_x,Q_A)
\leq\frac{4V_A F_R}{4V_A+F_R}.
$$

The right-hand side is defined as zero when $V_A=F_R=0$. For pure outputs this is equivalent, when $V_A>0$, to

$$
v\geq\frac{V_A^2}{V_A+F_R/4}.
$$

Substituting the exact design value $v=V_A/(D+1)$ proves the lower bound. The [proof](reference_bound.md) uses QFI data processing with an adjustable comparison generator. The finite-reference metrology ingredients are established prior work and are attributed in the [source comparison](../references/RELATED_WORK.md#finite-reference-metrology).

Second, for every integer $K\geq1$, a finite conserving measurement prepares

$$
|\psi_{N,\phi}\rangle
=\sum_{i=1}^{D}\sqrt{N_i/K}\,e^{i\phi_i}|i\rangle,
\qquad
N\sim\mathrm{Multinomial}(K;1/D,\ldots,1/D),
$$

with independent third-root phases and reference QFI $4(K-1)V_A$. At $K=D+1$, ordinary multinomial moments give the exact design tensor. The nontrivial implementation step is matching those moments to the charge-sector structure of an independent reference. The [construction](conserving_construction.md) makes that match explicit, including the complete POVM, neutral pointer, and compression of repeated reference charges.

If $V_A=0$, the system charge is scalar. Every measurement on $B$ is then compatible with its charge, and a one-dimensional reference suffices. The minimum remains zero. The case $D=1$ is included.

## 6. Related targets and reading route

The same effective measurement on $B$ has effects $E_x=Dp_x\rho_x^{\mathsf T}$. Exact designs are therefore equivalent to tight rank-one informationally complete POVMs, using the established characterization of Scott. At the maximally mixed state, they are also Fisher symmetric in the sense explained by Zhu and Hayashi. The [measurement-geometry page](measurement_geometry.md) gives the derivation and the precise statistical normalization.

Approximation requires a separate statement of the error criterion. The [accuracy page](accuracy.md) proves the achievable additive moment error, gives a balanced-charge counterexample for the usual multiplicative moment condition, and quantifies the effect of mixed output states. Two-sided relative Fisher isotropy within the exact rank-one class has a different, nearly matched resource bound. A one-sided guarantee of statistically competitive information is cheaper.
